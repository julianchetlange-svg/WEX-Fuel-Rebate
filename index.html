Paid in full tri-monthly (10th, 20th, 1st business day) via Direct Debit. Applied to all Monthly Retail Transactions.
How this is calculated. Consumption tier is set by total program gallons for the month (all locations combined, all gallon-measured products including DEF). The tier rate and the 0.20% early-pay rate are each applied to that month's eligible spend (Net Cost). Each location's share is its own spend × the program-wide rate for that month. Assumes all contract conditions were met each month (central billing, paid in full within 26 days). The first tier is shown as 90 bp — the contract image reads "(0.90%)"; it doesn't affect results because every month landed in the top tier.
